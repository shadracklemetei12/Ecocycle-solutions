Impact in Action

From schools to corporations, here are some of the recycling projects we've successfully delivered.

Education

School Recycling Program

Implemented a comprehensive recycling program across 10 schools, installing dedicated recycling bins and training students on waste sorting. Achieved a 40% reduction in school waste going to landfills.

Corporate

Corporate Waste Audit

Conducted a comprehensive audit of corporate waste management practices for major businesses, identifying inefficiencies. Helped companies reduce their waste output by 30% through improved sorting and recycling protocols.

Community

Community Clean-Up Drive

Organized community clean-up drives across local neighborhoods, engaging over 200 volunteers. Successfully cleaned 500+ acres of public land and collected 700 kgs of waste from parks and public spaces.
