About Us

The people, purpose, and passion behind EcoCycle Solutions.

Who We Are

Our Story & Values

Founded in 2026 with a shared vision of environmental responsibility and circular economy innovation.

🌱 Our Purpose

To build trust and credibility while creating a measurable, positive environmental impact through responsible recycling and waste management across Kenya.

📖 Our Story

Founded in 2026 by Stephen Roy, Stanley Adero, Shadrack Lemetei, and Peter Mwangangi to address growing plastic waste challenges. We transform waste into valuable resources through circular economy practices.

🎯 Our Mission

To create a cleaner, greener future through responsible recycling — reducing landfill pollution, conserving natural materials, and creating green employment opportunities for communities.

🔭 Our Vision

To become the leading recycling partner in East Africa, delivering lasting environmental and social impact for present and future generations through innovation and excellence.
